# Behavioral Practice

A 30-day pass through behavioural interview frames. Two stories per dimension, each with situation, action, result, and a learned lesson. End able to map an unfamiliar question to a relevant story in under 10 seconds.

- Horizon: 30d
- Outcome: Have a STAR-shaped story for every Amazon-style leadership principle, with one self-aware "what I would do differently".
- Tracks: Behavioral & Communication

Work each milestone as a claim that needs evidence. Predict the mechanism and
failure mode first, study the linked concepts, complete the drills without
copying an answer, and preserve the resulting code, measurements, or design.

## Milestone 1: Days 1-10 — Self & ownership

Leadership, ownership, customer obsession, innovation.

### Concepts

- [Leadership](https://learn.significanthobbies.com/curriculum/concepts/leadership-and-influence) — Driving outcomes through others.
- [Ownership](https://learn.significanthobbies.com/curriculum/concepts/ownership-and-accountability) — End-to-end responsibility.
- [Customer Obsession](https://learn.significanthobbies.com/curriculum/concepts/customer-obsession) — User-centered tradeoffs.
- [Innovation](https://learn.significanthobbies.com/curriculum/concepts/innovation-and-creativity) — Novel solutions, experimentation.



## Milestone 2: Days 11-20 — Working with people

Teamwork, conflict, communication.

### Concepts
