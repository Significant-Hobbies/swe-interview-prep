## The mechanism

SEO is matching what people are searching for and earning the trust to rank for it through good content and credible links. On-page basics (title, H1, page speed) are necessary but not sufficient — the real fight is content depth and inbound links.

## Expected evidence

A short audit table + 3 prioritized fixes with a rationale.

## Hints

- Title tag is the single biggest on-page lever.
- LCP is usually one bad image away from green.
