## The mechanism

Human review is a risk-control system. Route uncertain or high-impact cases, give reviewers evidence and clear rubrics, measure agreement, and feed corrections into evals.

## Expected evidence

A design outline with riskRouting, reviewRubric, feedbackLoop plus an explicit failure mode or trade-off.

## Hints

- Name the failure mode before selecting the mechanism.
