# STAR: leadership story

> Write a 4-paragraph STAR (Situation / Task / Action / Result) about a time you led without authority. Quantify the result.

Canonical: https://learn.significanthobbies.com/curriculum/drills/star-leadership

Write a 4-paragraph STAR (Situation / Task / Action / Result) about a time you led without authority. Quantify the result.

## The mechanism

Leadership without authority is getting people aligned on an outcome when you cannot order them to do it. The real signal is sustained behavior change in others over weeks — not the meeting where they nodded along.

## Expected evidence

starStory() returns { situation, task, action, result, metric: { name, unit, before, after }, influenceTactic (how you got compliance without authority) }. The checker enforces structure only: four distinct sections of 20+ words, action the longest, 150+ words total, before ≠ after, and the result section must name the metric and state its after value. Aim for 250-350 words — that is the interview bar, not the checker’s.

## Hints

- Leadership without authority = persuasion + alignment, not org charts.
- Cut adjectives; keep verbs and numbers.
