# Behavioral & Communication

The interview round that is not about code: influence, conflict, ownership, prioritisation, and learning from failure.

This track contains 10 connected concepts. Mastery means explaining each
mechanism, predicting its failure modes, and supporting decisions with code,
measurements, or a reviewable design artifact.

## Roadmaps

- [Behavioral Practice](https://learn.significanthobbies.com/curriculum/roadmaps/behavioral-practice) — Have a STAR-shaped story for every Amazon-style leadership principle, with one self-aware "what I would do differently".

## Concepts

- [Leadership](https://learn.significanthobbies.com/curriculum/concepts/leadership-and-influence) (core) — Driving outcomes through others.
- [Conflict Resolution](https://learn.significanthobbies.com/curriculum/concepts/conflict-resolution) (core) — Disagreement handling.
- [Decision Making](https://learn.significanthobbies.com/curriculum/concepts/problem-solving-and-decision-making) (core) — Tradeoffs under uncertainty.
- [Collaboration](https://learn.significanthobbies.com/curriculum/concepts/teamwork-and-collaboration) (intro) — Cross-functional work.
- [Failure & Learning](https://learn.significanthobbies.com/curriculum/concepts/failure-and-learning) (intro) — Owning mistakes, post-mortem.
- [Communication](https://learn.significanthobbies.com/curriculum/concepts/communication) (intro) — Written/verbal, audience adaptation.
- [Prioritization](https://learn.significanthobbies.com/curriculum/concepts/time-management-and-prioritization) (intro) — Eisenhower, OKR alignment.
- [Innovation](https://learn.significanthobbies.com/curriculum/concepts/innovation-and-creativity) (core) — Novel solutions, experimentation.
- [Customer Obsession](https://learn.significanthobbies.com/curriculum/concepts/customer-obsession) (intro) — User-centered tradeoffs.
- [Ownership](https://learn.significanthobbies.com/curriculum/concepts/ownership-and-accountability) (intro) — End-to-end responsibility.
