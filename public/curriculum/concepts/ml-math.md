## Practice

### Dot product and matmul shapes

Vectors a=[1,2,3], b=[4,5,6]. Compute dot(a,b). Matrices A (2×3) and B (3×2): is A·B valid? What is the output shape?

**Expected evidence:** dot=32; A·B is 2×2.
