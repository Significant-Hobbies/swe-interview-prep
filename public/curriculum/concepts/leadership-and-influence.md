# Leadership

Driving outcomes through others.

- Difficulty: core
- Tracks: Behavioral & Communication, Behavioral & Communication

## Mental model

Leadership without authority is getting people aligned on an outcome when you cannot order them to do it. The real signal is sustained behavior change in others over weeks — not the meeting where they nodded along.

## Practice

### STAR: leadership story

Write a 4-paragraph STAR (Situation / Task / Action / Result) about a time you led without authority. Quantify the result.

**Expected evidence:** starStory() returns { situation, task, action, result, metric: { name, unit, before, after }, influenceTactic (how you got compliance without authority) }. The checker enforces structure only: four distinct sections of 20+ words, action the longest, 150+ words total, before ≠ after, and the result section must name the metric and state its after value. Aim for 250-350 words — that is the interview bar, not the checker’s.

## Review prompts

- The room agreed and nothing changed two weeks later. What is the actual evidence that you influenced anyone?
