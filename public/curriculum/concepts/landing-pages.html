Landing Pages

Turning a visitor into an activated user: hero, proof, single CTA.

go-to-marketpositioning

Mental model

A landing page has one job: get the right visitor to take one action. Headline that says what it is and who it is for, one clear call to action, social proof, no distractions. If a visitor cannot tell what the product does in 3 seconds, you have lost them.

How to study Landing Pages

Begin by restating the mental model in your own words, then connect it to a concrete system you have built or operated. Name the mechanism, the constraint it addresses, and the trade-off it introduces. Use Julian Shapiro — Landing Page Copywriting (Startup Handbook), Marketing Examples — Landing page teardowns to check details, but close the source before writing your explanation. Retrieval is the learning step; rereading is only preparation.

Next, compare Landing Pages with SEO, Product Analytics. Ask what changes in correctness, latency, resource use, operability, and failure recovery. Complete Critique a real landing page and preserve the command, input, output, and one failed attempt as evidence. Finish by explaining the idea without jargon to someone who has not studied the track.

Proof of understanding

  • Explain the mechanism from first principles and identify the state it reads or changes.
  • Give one situation where the concept is the right choice and one where it is not.
  • Predict a realistic failure mode before running the drill, then compare the prediction with evidence.
  • Connect the result to a roadmap or build artifact instead of treating the concept as isolated trivia.

Practice and explain it back

Critique a real landing page

Pick a real landing page (not your own). Score it against: clear hero (3s rule), specific audience, single primary CTA, social proof, friction-free signup. Write a rewrite for the hero section.

Expected evidence: A scoring rubric (1-5 per criterion) + a rewritten hero (headline + sub + CTA).

Build evidence

Synthesize: Application Engineering

Turn backend, client, UX, real-time, interactive, analytics, and distribution skills into one complete product. Produce one working system, benchmark, or evidence-backed design that integrates the path.

  • Implements or precisely models the core mechanisms from all three milestones
  • Includes at least one injected failure or adversarial case and demonstrates recovery
  • Reports quality, latency, resource, reliability, or usability measurements relevant to the domain
  • Ships a concise architecture note explaining decisions, trade-offs, and remaining risks
